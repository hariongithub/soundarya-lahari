Soundarya Lahari, attributed to Adi Shankaracharya: all 100 verses in Devanagari, Telugu and IAST. Verses 1 to 5 have a plain meaning and word-by-word gloss; 6 to 100 are being added.

How the verse fits the Shikharini meter

Every line of these verses has 17 syllables, each either heavy or light, in one fixed order called Shikharini, with a pause after the sixth syllable. A syllable is heavy if its vowel is long, or if a conjunct consonant, anusvāra or visarga follows it. Below is line 1 of verse 1. Open the Meter tab to tap through every verse.

Readings differ between editions. Verses 6 to 100 are taken from a Telugu ebook, compared between its two sections and checked against the metre; each verse carries a badge showing how sure the text is, and some lines still need proofreading against a printed book.
