How the verse fits the Shikharini meter

Every line of these five verses has 17 syllables, each either heavy or light, in one fixed order called Shikharini, with a pause after the sixth syllable. A syllable is heavy if its vowel is long, or if a conjunct consonant, anusvāra or visarga follows it. Below is line 1 of verse 1. Open the Meter tab to tap through every verse.
